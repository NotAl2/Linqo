Backups are snapshots, not continuous sync, and depend on storage, connectivity and provider availability. Keep a portable local backup of important saves. Google storage protection is not a claim of end-to-end encryption.

Use the same Google account to access its backups. Signing out or deleting a Linqo account does not erase the device library or personal Drive snapshots. See the privacy policy for controls, retention and deletion.
