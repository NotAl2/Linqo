Purchases use Cashfree hosted web checkout for Indian payments in INR. Start checkout in Linqo while signed into Google. Linqo creates a server-priced order bound to that Google identity; no manual purchase code is needed. A payment email, screenshot, redirect or caller-provided account ID cannot claim a tier. Sign in with the same Google identity and choose Restore purchase after reinstalling or switching devices. XL to XXL is a separate **₹649** purchase, without proration initially. Buy Me a Coffee remains for voluntary donations only; donations do not unlock either tier.

Linqo grants a tier only for a verified successful payment matching its own order, exact INR amount and payment currency. Pending, failed, uncaptured, unrelated and sandbox payments do not grant paid access. Repeated events and orders do not stack quotas. API verification on restoration can recover missed webhook delivery. Provider outages may delay an unlock; keep the Cashfree order reference for private support.

Existing signed unlocks work offline. Online checks apply completed refunds: a fully refunded payment loses its unlock, falling back to another valid XL/XXL purchase or Free. Successful partial refunds are accumulated by payment ID; they revoke the unlock once the whole product price has been refunded. Cancelled refunds do not revoke access; delayed captures cannot erase refund records. All existing saves remain readable, editable, searchable, extractable, exportable and deletable above the resulting quota. New additions are blocked only in a later qualified build with limits enabled.

Sandbox is clearly marked and grants no production tier. Live domestic payments require account/product activation, credentials, approved checkout domain and acceptance testing. International/USD purchases are deferred; current prices are fixed INR and Linqo does not apply a live dollar conversion.
